IN THE LOOP

A glimpse of connected work.

This interactive concept shows Goblin at the center of everyday development conversations. Every exchange is scripted; nothing is sent to an external service.

The demo imagines channels including Teams, Discord, and email. Their appearance here does not mean those integrations are currently available.

Choose a channel or a different ask, scrub the timeline, or press Space to pause. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
